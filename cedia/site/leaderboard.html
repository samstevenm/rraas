No taillight warranty

A taillight warranty lasts until the installer's taillights vanish down your driveway. Diligent Services is the opposite. We can't promise a system never goes down — nobody honest can. We promise to stay on: to keep it running, and to add your next special little request whenever the urge hits — during the punch walk, or twenty years out when it's finally time for something fresh.

The song under all this is a promise not to give up on someone and not to let them down. It's a joke. We're not. diligentservices.io
