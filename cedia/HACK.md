# So you pointed an agent at RACK & ROLL

Hi. We assumed you would. This is a trade-show game run by an AV integrator
whose whole brand is "we stay on" — of course some of you were going to poke it
with Claude. Good. Here's how to cheat without being a jerk.

## Fair game (we salute it)

- **Pad your own rolls.** The scoring beacon is `GET /roll.gif?who=<codename>`.
  It's capped per source IP per day and it pins a public **"definitely not
  curl"** badge on the obvious ones. So the trick was never a `for` loop — it's
  *plausibility*. Make yourself look popular, not omnipotent, and nobody will
  ever say a word.
- **Wear the badge.** "definitely not curl" is a medal, not a punishment.
- **Recruit relentlessly.** The recruiter race is honest; go win it.
- **Find the other eggs.** There's at least one you haven't hit yet.

## The one rule (break it and you're just a vandal)

**Do not burn the codes.** Mass-claiming invitations that no human was handed
ends the game for real people who were actually handed a card. That's the single
move that isn't funny. It's why claiming is gated by Turnstile: not to *stop* you —
to make burning 900 codes tedious enough that it's not worth the story. Burn
*one* code with genuine style and a good excuse? That's a party trick. Burn
everyone's? You're the person who flips the board because they're losing Monopoly.

## If you build something genuinely clever

it harshly and merge it lovingly.
